About me

My Story

I started by building what users could see. Then I got curious about everything running behind it.

Where I Started

My technical journey began in frontend engineering, mostly HTML and CSS. My focus was the structure and presentation of what users interact with. That work still matters to me: it taught me to care about the person on the other side of the screen.

Why I Moved Into Cloud

With more exposure to technology, my questions changed. How does a website reach users? How do servers communicate? How are networks structured? How does code move from development to production? How do cloud systems stay secure and available? How can infrastructure be automated?

Those questions pushed me toward Linux, networking and AWS. From there I explored Git-based workflows, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform and Ansible. Security became part of the path because infrastructure is not only about making systems work; it is about protecting the systems, identities, applications and data inside them.

What I Am Doing Now

I am developing my skills across AWS, cloud networking, IAM, Linux administration, cybersecurity fundamentals, Docker, Kubernetes, Amazon EKS, Terraform, Ansible, CI/CD, monitoring and Git/GitHub, and I use Jira to organise the work.

My Technical Focus

These tools make more sense together than apart. AWS provides the cloud infrastructure and networking lets its parts communicate. IAM controls who and what has access. Linux runs underneath the workloads. Docker packages applications and Kubernetes manages the containers. Terraform provisions infrastructure from code, while Ansible handles configuration. CI/CD automates delivery, and Prometheus, Grafana and Datadog show how everything behaves. Security principles run through all of it.

WHERE I'M HEADING

I plan to keep growing at the intersection of cloud engineering, DevOps, cloud security, infrastructure automation and DevSecOps. That means continuing to deepen my expertise, building stronger production-ready skills and gaining practical experience, growing toward secure DevOps and DevSecOps environments.

My Approach to Engineering

I build things to understand them. I prefer small working projects over theory alone, I try to design with least privilege and visibility from the start, and I document what I learn so the next iteration is better.
